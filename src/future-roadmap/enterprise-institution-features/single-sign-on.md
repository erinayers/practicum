# Single Sign-On

## User Story

As an institution admin,  
I want users to sign in with the institution's identity provider,  
so that account access follows existing school or company policies.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Institution can configure SSO.
- Users can sign in through the identity provider.
- Access can be limited to approved domains or groups.
- SSO integrates with classroom and marketplace access.

## Paywall Guidance

SSO should be paid because it is an enterprise operations feature.
