# LMS Gradebook Export

## User Story

As an instructor,  
I want to export lab completion results to an LMS gradebook,  
so that Practicum fits into existing course workflows.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Instructor can export grades or completion data.
- Export includes student, lab, score, and timestamp.
- Export format is compatible with common LMS workflows.
- Future integrations can support direct LMS sync.

## Paywall Guidance

Manual local export could be included in a classroom plan. Direct LMS integrations should be paid because they require maintenance and institution support.
