# Sell Paid Lab Spec

## User Story

As a lab creator,  
I want to sell a high-quality lab spec,  
so that I can earn revenue from instructional content.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Creator can mark a listing as paid.
- Marketplace handles payment.
- Buyer receives access to the purchased spec.
- Purchased specs can be imported into the local Practicum app.
- Refund and license policies are visible before purchase.

## Paywall Guidance

Paid lab sales are a marketplace feature and should be monetized. The local app should still support importing specs from disk, including free specs.

## Product Notes

Consider creator tiers later:

- Individual creator
- Verified educator
- Institution publisher
- Commercial training provider
