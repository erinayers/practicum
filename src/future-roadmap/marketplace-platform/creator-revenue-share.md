# Creator Revenue Share

## User Story

As the Practicum platform owner,  
I want to collect a percentage of paid marketplace sales,  
so that the platform can fund ongoing open-source development.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Marketplace records gross sale amount.
- Marketplace calculates creator payout.
- Marketplace calculates Practicum platform fee.
- Creator can view payout history.
- Platform fee is disclosed clearly.

## Suggested Fee Model

- Start with a simple percentage fee on paid listings.
- Consider lower fees for open-source contributors or verified education partners.
- Avoid excessive fees that discourage high-quality creators.

## Paywall Guidance

Revenue-share infrastructure is paid platform functionality. It should fund the open-source core without locking down the core app.
