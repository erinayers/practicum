# Publish Free Lab Spec

## User Story

As a lab creator,  
I want to publish a free lab spec to the marketplace,  
so that other instructors and learners can discover and use it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Creator can upload a valid lab spec.
- Marketplace validates the spec before publishing.
- Listing includes title, description, author, version, tags, difficulty, and license.
- Users can download or open the spec in Practicum.

## Paywall Guidance

Free public publishing can remain free to encourage ecosystem growth. Advanced creator analytics and private distribution can be paid.
