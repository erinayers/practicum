# Student Roster Management

## User Story

As an instructor,  
I want to manage student rosters,  
so that lab access and progress are tied to the right learners.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Instructor can invite students.
- Instructor can remove students.
- Instructor can group students by course or section.
- Roster controls integrate with lab assignments.

## Paywall Guidance

Roster management should be paid for classrooms and institutions. The local app should not require a roster for ordinary use.
